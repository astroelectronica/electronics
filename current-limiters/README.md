# Current Limiters

A current monitor (also known as a current sensor or current monitor system) is a device that detects and measures the flow of electrical current through a conductor. It converts the measured current into a readable electrical signal—such as voltage, a proportional current, or a digital output—allowing systems to display, log, or react to the power levels for safety, control, or energy management.
